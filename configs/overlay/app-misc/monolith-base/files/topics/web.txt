web — browsing and fetching from the console

  lynx is the browser on this disc (www-client/lynx): text-mode, no
  images, works the same over a serial console as any other TTY program.

      lynx https://example.org

  www-client/w3m was tried for this disc and dropped — it fails to build
  against this musl toolchain — so lynx is what shipped instead.

  Anything here that speaks HTTP (lynx, curl, wget) honors the standard
  proxy variables:

      export http_proxy=http://proxy.example:3128/
      export https_proxy=$http_proxy

  curl and wget also cover scripted fetches (see: monolith-advisory-check
  for a worked example of curl-with-fallback-to-wget).
